# Fleet economics of electric delivery vans
_Illustrative synthetic document for demonstration. Figures are fictional._

Over a seven-year horizon, the total cost of ownership of a battery-electric delivery van is projected to be 12% lower than a diesel equivalent. The savings come mainly from fuel and maintenance costs. The purchase price premium for an electric van averages 35% over the diesel model. Maintenance costs per kilometre are about 40% lower because electric drivetrains have fewer moving parts.
