# Range and daily operations
_Illustrative synthetic document for demonstration. Figures are fictional._

Real-world van range averages 250 km, while the typical urban delivery route is 140 km. Winter range drops by up to 25% in cold weather, which can push long routes beyond a single charge. Route planning tools should reserve a 20% energy buffer on every route. Payload is reduced by roughly 150 kg because of battery weight.
