# Depot charging infrastructure
_Illustrative synthetic document for demonstration. Figures are fictional._

A depot with 40 vehicles needs roughly 20 AC chargers of 11 kW plus 4 DC fast chargers for rapid turnaround. Grid connection upgrades take 9 to 18 months and are the most common cause of project delay. Smart charging software can cut peak demand charges by shifting load to overnight hours. Installed cost per depot charger varies widely with site electrical capacity.
